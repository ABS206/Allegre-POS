Student Practice Missions

Service Communication Checklist

Use these checkpoints during practice:

  1. Confirm the table number and guest count before entering items.
  2. Enter each item accurately and record modifiers clearly.
  3. Flag allergies or critical special requests before sending the order.
  4. Send the order to the kitchen and verify the ticket appears on the KDS.
  5. Kitchen acknowledges the ticket by selecting Start Preparing.
  6. Kitchen verifies the completed plate and selects Mark Ready.
  7. FOH confirms the correct table and serves ready items.
  8. Complete simulated checkout and close the table.
Reflection: Which task required the most accuracy? How could an error affect guest service and kitchen operations?
